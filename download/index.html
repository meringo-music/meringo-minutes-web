What it needs

  • A Mac with Apple Silicon. It won't run on an Intel Mac.
  • macOS 27 or later. If you haven't updated yet, it won't open.
  • Ollama, a free app. The setup assistant finds it or installs it for you.

What it downloads

WhatSize
The app84 MB
Speech models, on first useabout 710 MB
Ollama, a free appabout 600 MB

In all, the default setupabout 15 GB

Memory

I've measured it only on an M4 with 24 GB. I don't know the minimum yet, and I won't guess one.

$79 during the first month after launch, then $99. You pay once, and there's no subscription. The date the $79 ends will be printed here from launch day.

The free version (planned)
